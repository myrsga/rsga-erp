Snapshots are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB) — they are not uploaded anywhere. Guardian is read-only by default — it never writes or deletes on its own. The one exception is the manual Restore button, which only runs when you explicitly confirm it twice (including typing "RESTORE").

Jab tak ye tab/app khula hai, ye har 5 minute me khud-b-khud backup leta rehta hai (tab wapas open karne par ya internet wapas aane par bhi turant ek backup ho jaata hai). Agar Home Screen par Install karke rakha jaye to Android/Chrome kabhi-kabhi ise band hone ke baad bhi thodi der background me chala sakta hai — lekin koi bhi browser (iOS Safari sahit) ye guarantee nahi deta ki app poori tarah band hone par bhi backup chalta rahe. Sabse surakshit tareeka: din me ek-do baar Guardian khol lena, aur kabhi-kabhi "Export all" se poora history apne phone me save kar lena.

Naya/gaya data: har backup par pichle backup se exact record-level diff nikalta hai (kaun sa roll/admission/ID add hua, kaun sa gaya) — "What's new / what's missing" panel me dikhta hai.
Duplicate check: roll no., admission no., email, phone jaise fields par ek hi value do alag records me mile to "Duplicate records" panel me flag hota hai (data loss nahi maana jaata, sirf warning).
Retention: Ledger (counts/alerts/diffs/duplicates ka chhota record) aur Golden baseline hamesha ke liye save rehte hain. Full raw snapshot sirf naye 60 backups YA pichle 45 din (jo bhi zyada ho) tak poora rehta hai — usse purane sirf halke (counts/diffs/duplicates) reh jaate hain, poori history ka trend kabhi nahi khota.
Genuine drop? Agar koi drop galti se nahi balki sahi hai (jaise promotion/graduation ke baad records hataye gaye), to alert banner me "Accept new baseline" tap karke usse naya normal maan sakte ho.
